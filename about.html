About Us

At Luckin Consol, we are committed to delivering reliable, cost-effective, and customer-focused logistics solutions across global markets. With a strong presence in LCL Consolidation Services, we connect businesses through efficient supply chain management and seamless cargo movement. Backed by industry expertise and a passionate team, we specialize in handling import shipments with precision, transparency, and speed. Our operational excellence and personalized service approach have helped us build long-term relationships with customers and partners worldwide. We provide end-to-end logistics solutions tailored to the evolving needs of international trade, including:

  • LCL Consolidation Services
  • Ocean Freight Solutions
  • Air Freight Services
  • Dangerous Goods (DG) Handling
  • Customs Coordination & Documentation
  • Door Delivery & Distribution Support
